# Privacy Policy — Maică-ta Clicker

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Maică-ta Clicker ("the app") is a single-player offline game. This policy
explains what data the app does and does not handle.

## Data we collect

**We collect no personal data.** The app does not require an account, does not
ask for a name or email, and does not access your contacts, location, camera,
microphone, photos, or any other sensitive permission.

## What the app stores

The game saves your progress (currency, upgrades, achievements) **locally on
your device** using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and
is deleted if you uninstall the app or use the in-game "Reset game" option.

## Network access

The game does not transmit any gameplay or personal data over the network. It
is fully playable offline.

## Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Children

The app contains no harmful content and collects no personal information, so it
is suitable for all ages.
